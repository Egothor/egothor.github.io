    <div class="warm-copy">
      <p class="section-kicker">Human-centred engineering</p>
      <h2>Professional judgement still belongs inside technical systems.</h2>
      <p>Egothor projects are intentionally rigorous, but they are not built as cold abstractions. Search, assurance and cryptographic delivery all depend on the people who operate, review and trust the system.</p>
      <p>That is why the work emphasizes clear state models, reviewable evidence and operationally realistic design instead of fashionable complexity.</p>
