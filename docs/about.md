  <h1>About Egothor</h1>
  <p class="page-lede">Egothor is a research-driven engineering initiative focused on building systems where correctness, security, determinism and long-term operational value are non-negotiable. Its current work is maintained independently by Leo Galamboš.</p>
</section>

<section class="section shell section--tight-top">
  <div class="about-principles">
    <article><span class="about-symbol">◇</span><div><h2>Engineering model</h2><p>Determinism, runtime simplicity and evidence over assertion.</p></div></article>
    <article><span class="about-symbol">◈</span><div><h2>Operational focus</h2><p>Designed for real-world constraints, audit and regulatory pressure.</p></div></article>
    <article><span class="about-symbol">⬡</span><div><h2>Open by default</h2><p>Open-source projects, transparent process and reproducible builds wherever the project model permits it.</p></div></article>
  </div>
</section>

    <div class="warm-copy">
      <p class="section-kicker">Working style</p>
      <h2>Technical depth, with people and decisions kept visible.</h2>
      <p>Egothor is interested in systems that can survive review: by engineers, operators, security teams and auditors. That is as much a communication problem as a coding problem.</p>
      <p>The design preference is therefore explicit architecture, readable evidence and operational clarity — systems that serious professionals can interrogate rather than simply “trust the magic”.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section section--tinted">
  <div class="shell editorial-grid">
    <div><p class="section-kicker">The lineage</p><h2>Research questions become working systems.</h2></div>
    <div class="prose-column"><p>Egothor began in 1998 with full-text search and a language-independent stemming approach. It later expanded into distributed systems, crawling, network geolocation, sensor integration, experimental database technology, cryptographic engineering and software assurance.</p><p>Today, Egothor is best understood not as a single product but as an engineering lineage: research questions become concrete software, are tested against operational constraints and are either evolved, replaced or preserved as historical work.</p><a class="text-link" href="/heritage/">View the heritage <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></div>
  </div>
</section>
